# Possible Project Questions

- if someone likes one song, which other songs in our dataset sound most like it? : this is the basic idea behind suggesting music based on sound
- do we get different suggestions when we compare just a few sound qualities instead of many? : shows how the recommender's choices affect its suggestions
- which sound qualities, like energy or danceability, change the suggestions the most? : helps decide what the recommender should pay attention to
- do similar-sounding songs tend to have similar Spotify stream counts? : shows whether sound-based suggestions also tend to match in popularity
- do the most-streamed songs tend to have a similar sound, or are they quite different? : helps a recommender balance popular songs with variety
- can we find similar-sounding songs by different artists? : helps the recommender introduce people to new artists
- can a less-streamed song sound similar to a popular one? : gives the recommender a way to suggest less-known tracks
- do tracks that rank high for Spotify streams also rank high for YouTube views? : helps compare recommendations across platforms using each platform's rankings
- what sound qualities are common in songs with lots of Spotify streams? : could help someone find popular songs with a sound they enjoy
- do songs fall into groups with similar sounds, and can those groups help us suggest tracks? : offers another way to find music that sounds similar
